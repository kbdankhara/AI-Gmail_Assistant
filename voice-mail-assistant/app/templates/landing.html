Questions

Does it read my mail all the time?

No. It only checks your inbox while the assistant is on, about every 30 seconds, and stops the moment you turn it off or close the tab.

Why an App Password?

An App Password lets the assistant reach Gmail without ever seeing your Google password. You can revoke it from your Google account at any time.

Which browsers work?

Chrome and Edge on desktop or Android, on a secure (https) page. Voice recognition isn't available in Firefox, and Safari support is limited.
