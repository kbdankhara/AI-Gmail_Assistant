1. What we collect and why

2. Your emails

Emails are never stored. When the assistant is on, our server fetches unread messages from Gmail on demand, passes them through to be read aloud, and keeps no copy.

If you have saved an AI key, the sender, subject and text of the email being read are sent to the AI provider you chose, using your own key, to produce a summary or a draft reply. That provider's terms apply to that processing.

3. Your voice

Speech recognition and speech output are done by your browser. Your browser may send audio to its own speech service (for example Google's in Chrome) to recognise it. We receive only the recognised text of what you say, and only what is needed to act on it.

4. Who we share data with

  • Google, to sign you in and to reach Gmail with your App Password.
  • The AI provider you choose, as described above.
  • Razorpay, to take payment for your plan.

We do not sell your data or use your emails for advertising.

5. Your choices

  • Download your data from Profile settings.
  • Delete your account from Profile settings. This erases your account, saved secrets and consent history.
  • Revoke an App Password at any time from your Google account.

6. Security

Secrets are encrypted at rest and all traffic uses HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting you, we will tell you.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will ask for your consent again. Questions: {{ cfg.SUPPORT_EMAIL }}.
