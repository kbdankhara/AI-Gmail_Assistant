Create a Gmail App Password

  1. Turn on 2-Step Verification for your Google account, if it isn't on already.
  2. Open Google's App Passwords page below and sign in again if asked.
  3. Under "App name", type {{ cfg.APP_NAME }} and select Create.
  4. Copy the 16-character password Google shows you. It's only shown once.
  5. Paste it into the App Password field here and save.
